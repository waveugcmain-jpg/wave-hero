FAQS

The practical bits, before we get going.

Want to talk about your brand?

01Can we use the videos in paid ads?

Yes. We share the winning videos with your ad team. Ad usage rights are confirmed in your campaign scope before you launch.

02What happens after the campaign?

You finish with your creator network, campaign content and a clear picture of what worked. We share the winning ideas and what to try next, so you can keep building on the momentum.

03Do we need to manage the creators?

For our done-for-you campaigns, we handle it. We find the creators, guide the content and manage posting. If you choose done-with-you, we build the plan alongside your team.

04What happens when a video takes off?

We work out why people loved it, then get more creators to try the idea. The best videos go to your ad team, too. One good idea can go a long way.

05Can you promise a viral video?

No one can promise that. We test new ideas, learn from the response and give your brand more chances to be seen. The goal is a channel that keeps getting better.
