FAQS

Everything you need to know before we build your next wave.

Want to talk about your brand?

01What is a UGC program?

We recruit real creators who create new accounts dedicated to your brand, then post about your brand consistently. We build the network, run the content and keep testing until we find what gets people to stop, watch and share.

02What exactly is a Wave campaign?

A 30–45 day UGC campaign where we build your creator network, run the content, test different creative ideas and find what gets your audience to pay attention.

03How many creators do you work with?

We typically work with 8, 10 or 20+ creators, depending on the campaign and how aggressively you want to test.

04Are these real creators?

Yes. We recruit real creators who fit your brand and audience. They create dedicated UGC pages and post about your brand consistently.

05How much content will we get?

Our standard campaign produces roughly 300–450 pieces of content across Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

06What happens if a video goes viral?

We don't just move on to the next video. We figure out why it worked, put more creators behind the idea and turn the winning format into more content. We also share strong winners with your paid team.

07Do you only make content, or do you handle distribution too?

Distribution is a big part of the program. We build the creator network, manage the posting strategy and create a constant stream of content around your brand.

08Can we use the winning videos for paid ads?

Yes. We identify strong-performing creatives and can pass the winners to your paid team to test and scale.

09What do we actually get at the end of the campaign?

You get more than a folder of videos. You get an active creator network, the content produced during the campaign, your hook/messaging/format learnings, winning creative and a clearer picture of what to test next.

10Do we need to manage the creators?

No. That's our job. We handle creator recruitment, coordination, content direction, posting and the testing process end to end.

11What if nothing goes viral?

We can't promise that a specific video will go viral. But we don't just post and hope. We keep testing, learning and engineering new creative until we find something that breaks through. The more we learn, the more shots we have at getting there.

12How is Wave different from a normal UGC agency?

Most UGC agencies are built around getting you a certain number of videos. Wave is built around finding what works. We use creators and content as an experimentation engine to find winning hooks, formats and messages — then keep pushing the winners.

13How do we get started?

Book a call. We'll look at your brand, ICP and category, then show you how we'd approach your first 30–45 days. We'll also put together sample creative and a distribution plan for your brand.
